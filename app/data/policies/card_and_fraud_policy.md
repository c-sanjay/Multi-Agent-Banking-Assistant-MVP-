# Card and Fraud Policy (demo document)

## Blocking a card
A customer can block a debit card at any time. Blocking is immediate and a replacement card is issued
within 7 working days. A blocked card cannot be unblocked online; the customer must contact the branch.

## Disputed transactions
Unauthorised transactions reported within 3 working days carry zero customer liability.
Reports within 4 to 7 working days have limited liability. Later reports are reviewed case by case.

## Fraud monitoring signals
The bank monitors unusual amounts, foreign merchants, odd hours, unrecognised devices,
and rapid repeated small charges (card testing).
