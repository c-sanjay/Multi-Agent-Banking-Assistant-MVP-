# Personal Loan Policy (demo document)

## Eligibility
A customer is eligible for a personal loan if KYC is VERIFIED, the credit score is 650 or above,
and total monthly EMI obligations including the new loan do not exceed 50% of monthly income (FOIR).
The maximum personal loan is 24 times the customer's monthly income.

## Interest rates
Indicative annual rates depend on credit score: 780+ gets 10.5%, 740-779 gets 11.5%,
700-739 gets 12.5%, and 650-699 gets 14.0%. Rates are indicative and subject to underwriting.

## Tenure
Personal loan tenure ranges from 12 to 60 months.

## Documents required
Photo ID, address proof, last 3 months salary slips, and last 6 months bank statements.

## Prepayment and foreclosure
Part-prepayment is allowed after 6 EMIs with no charge. Foreclosure within 12 months attracts a 3% fee;
after 12 months the fee is 1%.
